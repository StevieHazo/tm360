What happens next

  • TM360 checks the proposal against current programme criteria.
  • Missing evidence and information are identified.
  • A reusable grant readiness pack is prepared.
  • Fund-specific drafts and budgets are developed.
  • The artist checks, approves and submits the final application.
Privacy by design

The live version will need a privacy notice, retention rules, secure storage, explicit consent and a clear statement explaining who can access the information.
