About TM360

BACK ON THE ROAD. BETTER EQUIPPED.

Twenty years after first tour managing, Steve Hazelden is returning with wider operational experience and a clear purpose: make grassroots touring more sustainable.

Experience from every side

Steve has worked as a driver, merch person, tour manager and live music venue operator. He knows what happens on stage, backstage, at the merch desk, in the venue office and in the van at 2am.

After live music, Steve built a career in security management and operational leadership. TM360 brings those worlds together: practical touring knowledge, structured planning, risk management and people-first leadership.

The principles

  • Protect artist wellbeing
  • Respect artistic integrity
  • Keep the band's top line in view
  • Solve problems early
  • Be honest about costs and funding
  • Leave the tour stronger than it started

Mental health matters.

Steve is undertaking training with Tonic Rider to become a Mental Health First Aider. TM360 is not a therapy or clinical service. It is a touring partner committed to spotting pressure early, encouraging open conversations, planning responsibly and signposting professional support where needed.
