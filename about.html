About TM360

BACK ON THE ROAD. BETTER EQUIPPED.

Twenty years after first tour managing, Steve Hazelden is returning with wider operational experience and a clear purpose: make grassroots touring more sustainable.

Experience from every side

Steve has worked as a driver, merch person, tour manager and live music venue operator. He understands what happens on stage, backstage, at the merch desk, in the venue office and in the van after the show.

After live music, Steve built a career in security management and operational leadership. TM360 brings those worlds together: practical touring knowledge, structured planning, risk awareness and people-first leadership.

The principles

  • Protect artist wellbeing
  • Respect artistic integrity
  • Keep the band's top line in view
  • Solve problems early
  • Be honest about costs and funding
  • Leave the tour stronger than it started

Mental health matters.

Steve is undertaking Mental Health First Aider training with Tonic Rider. TM360 is not a therapy or clinical service. It is a touring partner committed to planning responsibly, recognising pressure, encouraging open conversations and signposting professional support where needed.
