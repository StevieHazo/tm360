About TM360

EXPERIENCE FROM EVERY SIDE OF THE TOUR.

Steve Hazelden has worked as a driver, merch person, tour manager and live music venue operator, followed by a career in security management and operational leadership.

Back on the road. Better equipped.

TM360 combines practical music-industry knowledge with structured planning, risk awareness and calm decision-making. The service is designed around the realities of grassroots touring, where budgets are tight and every person often carries several responsibilities.

Steve is also undertaking Mental Health First Aider training with Tonic Rider. TM360 is not a therapy service, but artist wellbeing is considered in planning and delivery.

TM360 principles

  • Protect wellbeing
  • Respect artistic integrity
  • Protect the band's top line
  • Be honest about funding and costs
  • Solve problems early
  • Leave the tour stronger
