The operational centre of the tour

TM360 becomes the central point for schedules, travel, venues, crew, money and day-to-day decisions. Artistic decisions stay with the artist. Commercial authority stays with the authorised representative. TM360 makes those decisions work on the road.

The TM360 principle

Solve the problem at the lowest appropriate level. Keep the right people informed. Protect the artist from unnecessary noise.

Tour advancing

Every venue. Every detail. Checked in advance.

TM360 confirms the operational information needed for each show before arrival and records it in the tour documentation.

  • Promoter and venue contacts
  • Address, access, parking and loading
  • Load-in, soundcheck, doors, stage and curfew
  • Technical specification, stage plot and input list
  • Backline, dressing rooms and hospitality
  • Dietary and access requirements
  • Guest lists, passes and merchandise
  • Settlement, payment and emergency contacts

Settlements and tour accounting

Clear records at the end of every night.

TM360 checks the agreed deal, reviews sales and deductions, records the final payment position, retains supporting evidence and updates the working tour accounts.

Specialist accountancy, legal or tax matters remain with the artist's appointed professional advisers.

  • Deal and payment check
  • Ticket sales and deductions
  • Merchandise commission
  • Final artist payment
  • Receipts and supporting paperwork
  • Discrepancy and outstanding-payment record
  • Tour float and expense reconciliation

Problems, changes and contingencies

Touring never goes entirely to plan.

  1. Establish the facts
  2. Assess safety, artist, show and budget impact
  3. Identify practical options
  4. Agree the decision with the right authority
  5. Communicate the change
  6. Record contractual and financial impact
  7. Update the working plan
