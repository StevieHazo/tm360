Funding Hub

MORE TOUR. LESS FINANCIAL RISK.

Funding can make ambitious tours possible, but finding the right scheme and presenting a credible plan takes work. TM360 helps artists navigate that process.

How support works

  1. Discovery: understand the artist, tour, timescale and objectives.
  2. Eligibility: identify potentially suitable schemes and check the published criteria.
  3. Planning: create a realistic route, budget and delivery plan.
  4. Application: help shape a clear, evidence-led submission.
  5. Delivery: if successful, help manage the tour against the agreed plan.
No false promises.

Funding is competitive and never guaranteed. TM360 helps artists understand opportunities and submit the strongest honest application they can. The funder makes the decision.

Funding-ready tours

A strong proposal connects artistic ambition with a credible audience plan, realistic costs, clear outcomes and responsible delivery. TM360 helps make those parts work together rather than treating the form as a last-minute exercise.

Questions artists ask

Can TM360 guarantee a grant?

No. No responsible adviser can guarantee an award. TM360 improves preparation and helps artists target schemes for which they may be eligible.

Can funding pay for a tour manager?

Some programmes may accept professional and delivery costs where they are eligible, proportionate and clearly connected to the proposed activity. Each scheme's current guidance must be checked.

When should we start?

Before dates and costs become fixed. Early planning gives more scope to build a viable route, gather evidence and meet application deadlines.
