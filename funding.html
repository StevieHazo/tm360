Funding Hub

FUNDING ROUTES WE HELP YOU NAVIGATE.

TM360 helps artists identify potentially relevant programmes, prepare reusable information, build budgets and complete application drafts. Current eligibility, deadlines and availability are always checked against the funder’s official guidance.

Independent funding support

TM360 is an independent tour management and application-support service. Displaying or referring to a funding organisation does not imply endorsement, partnership, funding or guaranteed eligibility. Programme availability, deadlines and requirements must be checked against the funder’s official guidance before an application is prepared. Artists remain responsible for checking, approving and submitting final applications.
