The commitment

  • Plan responsibly
  • Communicate openly
  • Respect boundaries
  • Recognise pressure
  • Signpost qualified help

TM360 offers

  • Wellbeing-conscious planning
  • Mental Health First Aid-informed support

  • Routes to appropriate support

TM360 does not offer

  • Therapy or diagnosis
  • Medical advice
  • Psychiatric or crisis care
  • A replacement for healthcare
Urgent support

For urgent mental-health help contact NHS 111. If there is an immediate risk to life, call 999 or attend A&E.
