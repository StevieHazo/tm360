Artist wellbeing

Wellbeing is part of the tour plan.

Touring can be rewarding and demanding. TM360 considers routing, rest, communication, access and pressure before, during and after the tour.

Working towards partnership with Tonic Music.

Steve is undertaking Mental Health First Aider training through Tonic Rider. A formal partnership is being explored and remains subject to agreement.

Visit Tonic Music

Before

Access needs, rest, pressure points, communication and emergency contacts.

During

Proportionate check-ins, practical adjustments and appropriate signposting.

After

Review pressure, recovery, lessons and improvements for the next tour.

TM360 is not a crisis or clinical service.

For urgent mental-health help contact NHS 111. If there is an immediate risk to life, call 999 or attend A&E.
