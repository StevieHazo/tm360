Artist wellbeing

Better tours start with better planning.

Long drives, disrupted sleep, financial pressure and constant change can build up quickly. TM360° treats wellbeing as part of professional tour management.

  • Realistic routing and rest planning
  • Clear communication and expectations
  • Accessibility and reasonable adjustments
