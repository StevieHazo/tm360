Real touring risks

Problems solved before they become showstoppers.

A good plan does more than organise dates. It protects the artist, the budget and the show when real life gets in the way.

Cashflow

Funding is secured, but the money has not arrived

Deposits, travel and accommodation still need paying. TM360° plans cashflow and contingency before commitments are made.

People

A key team member drops out

Illness, burnout or an unexpected change can jeopardise the run. TM360° builds resilience into schedules and resources from the outset.

Venues

Requirements change at the last minute

Technical or access surprises quickly become expensive. TM360° identifies and resolves issues long before doors open.

Wellbeing

The artist runs out of energy

A successful tour should not come at the cost of health. TM360° plans rest, recovery and sustainable workloads into delivery.
