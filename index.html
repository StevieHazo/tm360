Sustainable touring

Touring should build careers, not burn them out.

Artists should not have to choose between financial survival, personal wellbeing and creative authenticity. TM360° plans tours around long-term careers, not short-term pressure.

  • Protect artistic identity and decision-making
  • Build realistic budgets and touring plans
  • Reduce avoidable travel, pressure and waste
  • Create stronger foundations for future touring

Artist wellbeing

People come before the schedule.

Touring can be demanding, stressful and isolating. TM360° treats artist mental health and wellbeing as part of professional tour management, not an optional extra.

  • Realistic routing, travel and rest planning
  • Clear communication and healthy expectations
  • Accessibility and reasonable adjustments
  • Mental Health First Aid awareness through Tonic Rider training
