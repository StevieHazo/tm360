Promoter vs band

Keeps the two pots separate so nothing is counted twice. The promoter pays venue, support, marketing and the artist fee out of ticket money; the band pays its own van, hotels, fuel and per diems out of the fee and merch. Change who pays any cost below.

Receipt & invoice scanner

Snap a receipt at the pump, hotel desk or venue, or upload an invoice PDF. It reads the supplier, date, amount and type of cost, you check it, and it goes straight into the Tour Budget and the promoter / band split.
